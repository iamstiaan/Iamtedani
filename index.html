The Real Origin

CALLED INTO
EXISTENCE.

I was a drug addict who lost everything.

I lived on the streets. I lost my wife and my three children. My life completely tumbled. In the deepest, darkest point of that destruction, I wanted to vanish — disappear into nothing.

One day, broken and empty, I bumped into a little boy. "Hi," I said. He said "Hi."

I asked his name. "Conor," he replied. Then he looked straight at me and asked: "Who are you?"

That question hit harder than anything had in years. In that moment I asked myself the only question that still mattered: Who am I?

Without hesitation, with complete certainty, the boy answered: "Don't be silly, Tedani. You're Tedani."

A child I had never met named me with absolute truth. No filter. No judgment. Just pure recognition.

That single moment cracked me open. It stripped away every excuse, every lie, every layer of self-deception I had been hiding behind. From that day I developed a ruthless intolerance for comfortable lies.

I stopped trying to "manage" my broken life. I started architecting a new one. That's what I now do for others.
